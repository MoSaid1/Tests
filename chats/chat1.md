# Wazen Store — Complete Website UI/UX Redesign Brief

Design a premium, modern, highly polished e-commerce website redesign for **Wazen Store (Wazen.eg)**, an Egyptian online retailer specializing in home appliances, electrical appliances, electronics, televisions, personal-care appliances, and related products.

## 1. About the Website

Wazen is an Egyptian e-commerce store selling a wide range of branded home appliances and electronics.

The website is primarily focused on:

* Selling products online
* Helping customers discover products by category
* Helping users compare products and choose the right appliance
* Showing discounts and current prices
* Building trust through warranty, delivery, payment, and customer support
* Driving users toward Add to Cart / Purchase
* Supporting both large and small household appliances
* Providing physical branch information
* Providing a blog/content section

The redesign should feel like a **serious, modern, trustworthy Egyptian electronics retailer**, not a generic template-based e-commerce website.

Do NOT design any admin dashboard or backend interface.

The project is ONLY the customer-facing website.
