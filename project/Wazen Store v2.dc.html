The 2026 air conditioner guide: HP, inverter and running cost for every room size

Wazen technical team| 31 Aug 2026|11 min read
article hero image · 1600×700

Most people buy an air conditioner one size too large because a salesperson rounded up. Here is the arithmetic instead.

A room in Cairo needs roughly 600 BTU per square metre if it faces north and sits on a middle floor. Add 15% for a top floor, another 15% for wide western glazing, and 10% for every extra person who uses the room regularly. A 20 m² north-facing bedroom therefore needs about 12,000 BTU — a one-horsepower unit — while the same room under a roof slab moves to 1.5 HP.

What inverter changes

A conventional compressor runs at full power then stops. An inverter compressor slows down once the room reaches temperature and holds it there, which removes the temperature swing and cuts consumption by 30–40% over a summer of daily use. At current residential tariffs, the price gap on a 1.5 HP unit returns in roughly two seasons.

Buy for the room you have, not the flat you might move to. An oversized unit short-cycles, never dehumidifies properly, and costs more to run.
